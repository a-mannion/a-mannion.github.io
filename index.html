Aidan Mannion

Welcome! I'm a data scientist and language technology researcher with a PhD in applied machine learning. I specialise in advanced NLP and text mining, including generative AI and embedding-focused methods.

My work has focused primarily on evaluating the performance and reliability of LLMs as support for practitioners in generalist healthcare applications.

I completed my BSc. in Mathematical Sciences at the University of Galway, my MSc. in Industrial and Applied Mathematics at Université Grenoble Alpes, and my PhD at the LIG under the supervision of Profs. Didier Schwab and Lorraine Goeuriot.

Experience

Postdoctoral Research Engineer - PARTAGES

April 2025 - March 2026

Worked on the PARTAGES project, laureate of the France 2030 call for projects on generative AI. The project unites a consortium including research labs, private firms, and hospitals with the goal of developing sovereign AI tools for the automation of administrative tasks in the French healthcare system.

  • Carried out benchmarking and feasibility studies to identify the open-source AI solutions best suited to the French healthcare context.
  • Implemented large-scale post-training experiments to adapt LLMs for this domain - delivered a family of models for deployment in healthcare institutions.

Doctoral Researcher - LIG

September 2021 - December 2025

  • Thesis title: Demystifying Machine Learning Applications in General Medicine (available here).
  • CIFRE PhD - interdisciplinary industrial-academic collaboration between the LIG (Laboratoire d'Informatique de Grenoble) and SOS Médecins.
  • Trained custom foundation language models for clinical text in the specialised domain of unscheduled generalist consultations.
  • Fine-tuned a wide range of models for diagnosis prediction from these consultation records.
  • Carried out quantitative studies of the applicability of decision support tools based on these models in the SOS Médecins context.

Lead AI Engineer - EPOS SAS / SOS Médecins

November 2020 - December 2025

Established a clinical decision support system for SOS Médecins in collaboration with the project management team at the software provider EPOS.

  • Studied needs and workflow bottlenecks of practitioners along with technical feasibility to develop proof-of-concept experiments.
  • Built development and deployment tools for real-time decision support based on models and prediction frameworks arising from my doctoral research.
  • Set up automated deployment and monitoring for clinical decision support in production as part of the SOMELOG mobile application used by SOS Médecins practitioners.

Teaching Assistant - Université Grenoble Alpes

Research Intern - LIG

March 2020 - September 2020

Masters thesis project in machine learning & biomedical text mining, the goal of which was to develop predictive algorithms to help GPs working with SOS Médecins to more effectively treat patients.

  • Implemented a custom data processing pipeline for patient data from the mobile application used by these GPs, identifying clinically relevant information in order to build a dataset of relevant and usable electronic health records.
  • Designed an experimental paradigm for the comparison and validation of various machine learning methods to make inferences from this data (physiological measurements, pharmacological and medication-related information, observations and clinical reports).
  • Constructed a machine learning framework capable of predicting patients' likelihood of reaccess to the GP healthcare system and probability of serious illness & hospitalisation with ~95% precision.

Bioinformatics Data Scientist - LXRepair

June 2019 - August 2019

Summer internship as a statistician with a genetic research team working on proof-of-concept trials of novel diagnostic techniques for the prediction of patient response to radiotherapy based on DNA repair signatures.

  • Built a data ingestion pipeline to collate information from multiple clinical and experimental sources.
  • Trained and validated ML models (random forests, collaborative filtering) to find potential genetic biomarkers relevant to treatment response.
  • Presented my work to technical and non-technical audiences - contributed analyses to investor pitches and fundraising proposals.
